# Payments, your account and your data

Albert's Marketplace Help Centre · Customers · Version 1.0, effective 1 September 2026

## 1. How you pay

Albert's Marketplace accepts **payment by card only**: Visa, Mastercard and
American Express. We do not accept bank transfers, cash on delivery, cheques or
gift cards from other shops. Your card is charged when you place the order, for
the whole order, even if it is shipped by several sellers.

## 2. Discount codes and daily deals

A discount code is entered at checkout, and **only one code can be used per
order**. Codes cannot be exchanged for cash and do not apply to delivery costs.
Daily deals are price reductions shown on the product page for one day; the
price you see when you pay is the price you are charged.

## 3. If you were charged twice

A double charge is almost always a payment authorised twice by your bank, of
which only one is collected. If both appear as collected on your statement after
3 business days, report it from your order page with a screenshot of the
statement: we refund the second charge **within 3 business days** of your
report.

## 4. Your account

You need an account to place an order. One account per person, linked to one
email address. You can change your email address, your password and your saved
delivery addresses at any time in "My account".

If you forget your password, use "Forgot password" on the sign-in page: the
reset link is valid for **1 hour**.

## 5. Closing your account and deleting your data

You can close your account from "My account", under "Privacy". When you close
it:

- your account is deactivated at once, and you can no longer sign in;
- your personal data is **deleted within 30 days**, except what the law
  requires us to keep: invoices and payment records are kept for 10 years;
- your reviews remain published, without your name.

You can also ask for a **copy of your personal data** from the same page. We
send it within 30 days, as a file you can open in a spreadsheet.

For any other question about your data, write to our data protection officer
at privacy@albertsmarketplace.example.

# Returns and refunds

Albert's Marketplace Help Centre · Customers · Version 1.0, effective 1 September 2026

This page explains when you can return an item bought on Albert's Marketplace,
how to start a return, what it costs, and when you are refunded. It applies to
every order placed on Albert's Marketplace, whichever seller sold the item.

## 1. The return window

You can return an item within **21 days of its delivery date**. The delivery
date is the date shown as "Delivered" in your order history; if the carrier
recorded no delivery date, the window starts 7 days after the order date, which
is our estimated delivery date for every order.

The window is the same in all six countries we deliver to: France, Germany,
Italy, Spain, the Netherlands and Belgium. It is longer than the legal minimum
in these countries, and it does not reduce any right you have under the law of
your country.

A return request sent on the 21st day counts as on time, even if the parcel
reaches the seller later. A request sent on the 22nd day is refused
automatically, except in the cases described in section 4.

## 2. What you can return

You can return any item that is:

- unopened, with its original seal or wrapping intact;
- unused, in its original packaging;
- accompanied by all the parts it came with (applicators, refills, caps,
  brushes, travel pouches).

Gift sets can only be returned whole. If one item of a set is defective, see
section 4: you do not need to return the rest of the set.

## 3. What cannot be returned once opened

For reasons of hygiene and health, some products cannot be returned once their
seal has been broken or their packaging opened, because the seller cannot sell
them again and cannot check that they have not been contaminated. This applies
to:

- skincare products: creams, serums, oils, masks, cleansers and toners;
- make-up: foundations, concealers, lipsticks, lip glosses, mascaras, eyeliners
  and any product applied near the eyes or on the lips;
- hair-colouring products and treatments;
- perfumes and fragrances whose cap or seal has been removed;
- razors, razor heads, toothbrush heads and any product that touches the skin
  or the mouth directly;
- nail products that have been opened, including polishes, gels, glues and
  false nails.

This rule has one exception, and it matters: **an opened product can always be
returned if it is defective, damaged on arrival, not as described on its
product page, or caused a reaction that a doctor or pharmacist has confirmed.**
In those cases the 21-day window applies as usual, the return is free, and you
do not need the original packaging. Keep the product until the seller or our
support team has confirmed what to do with it: some sellers do not need it
back.

## 4. Defective, damaged or wrong items

If an item arrives damaged, does not work, is not what you ordered, or is
missing parts, tell us **within 48 hours of delivery** for damage visible on
arrival, and within the 21-day window for any other defect. Send at least one
photo of the item and, for damage on arrival, one of the parcel as it was
delivered.

For these items:

- the return shipping is free: we send you a prepaid label;
- you choose between a refund and a replacement, if the seller has stock;
- a missing part can be sent on its own, without returning the item.

If a product is suspected to be counterfeit, do not return it to the seller.
Report it from your order page with the reason "Not authentic". Our trust and
safety team examines it and refunds you directly if the report is upheld.

## 5. What a return costs

A return for any reason other than those in section 4 costs **€3.95**, deducted
from your refund. This covers the prepaid label and is the same for every
carrier and every country. You can instead send the item back at your own
expense with a tracked service; you then pay nothing to us, and you must enter
the tracking number in your return request.

## 6. How to start a return

1. Open your order history and select the order.
2. Choose "Return an item", the item, and the reason.
3. Print the prepaid label, or choose to send the item yourself.
4. Drop the parcel at any pick-up point of the carrier shown on the label.

The seller has **3 business days** from receiving the parcel to confirm the
return. If the seller does not answer within **7 business days** of receiving
it, Albert's Marketplace confirms the return on the seller's behalf: this is
our marketplace guarantee.

## 7. When you are refunded

Once the return is confirmed, you are refunded **within 5 business days** to the
card you paid with. Albert's Marketplace accepts payment by card only, so every
refund goes back to a card. Your bank may take a few more days to show it.

You are refunded the price of the item and, if the whole order is returned, the
original delivery cost. The €3.95 return cost of section 5 is deducted, unless
section 4 applies.

## 8. Late deliveries

An order that has not arrived **2 days after its estimated delivery date** is
considered late. You can then cancel it from your order history and be refunded
in full, delivery included, within 5 business days, without waiting for the
parcel. If the parcel arrives after you cancelled, refuse it or send it back
with the prepaid label we email you.

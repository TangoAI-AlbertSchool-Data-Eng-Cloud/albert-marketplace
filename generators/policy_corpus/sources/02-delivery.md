# Delivery

Albert's Marketplace Help Centre · Customers · Version 1.0, effective 1 September 2026

## 1. Where we deliver

Albert's Marketplace delivers to six countries: **France, Germany, Italy, Spain,
the Netherlands and Belgium**. We do not deliver to any other country,
including Switzerland, the United Kingdom and Luxembourg, and we do not deliver
to post-office boxes or to parcel lockers outside these six countries.

Every item is shipped by the seller who sells it. An order with items from
several sellers arrives in several parcels, possibly with different carriers,
and each parcel has its own tracking number.

## 2. Carriers

Parcels are carried by one of four carriers, chosen by the seller for each
parcel:

| Carrier | Tracking |
|---|---|
| Albatros Parcels | on the carrier's website, with the tracking number |
| Blue Heron Logistics | on the carrier's website, with the tracking number |
| Kestrel Freight | on the carrier's website, with the tracking number |
| Swift Otter Delivery | on the carrier's website, with the tracking number |

All four deliver to all six countries. You cannot choose the carrier.

## 3. How long delivery takes

Every order receives an **estimated delivery date 7 days after the order
date**. Most parcels arrive sooner: in our experience, between 2 and 9 days
after the order, depending on the seller and the carrier.

An order moves through three statuses in your order history:

- **Processing:** the seller is preparing your parcel;
- **In transit:** the carrier has the parcel;
- **Delivered:** the carrier has recorded the delivery.

## 4. What delivery costs

Delivery is **free for orders of €35 or more**, per seller. Below €35, delivery
costs **€4.90** per seller, in every country. The delivery cost is shown before
you pay.

## 5. If your parcel is late or lost

A parcel is **late** if it has not arrived 2 days after its estimated delivery
date. You can then cancel the order and be refunded in full, delivery included
(see "Returns and refunds", section 8).

A parcel is **lost** if the carrier has shown no movement for 10 days. Report
it from your order page: we refund you within 5 business days, or send a
replacement if the seller has stock, at your choice.

## 6. If your parcel arrives damaged

Report it **within 48 hours** of delivery, with a photo of the parcel and one of
the item. The return is free and you choose between a refund and a
replacement (see "Returns and refunds", section 4).

## 7. Changing the delivery address

You can change the address only while the order is **Processing**. Once the
order is **In transit**, the address can no longer be changed by us or by the
seller; contact the carrier directly with your tracking number.

# Selling on Albert's Marketplace

Albert's Marketplace Seller Centre · Sellers · Version 1.0, effective 1 September 2026

This policy applies to every independent seller who lists products on Albert's
Marketplace. By listing a product, you accept it.

## 1. Fees

| Fee | Amount |
|---|---|
| Commission on each sale | **12%** of the item price, delivery excluded |
| Monthly subscription | **€0** for sellers with fewer than 50 sales in the month; **€19** per month from the 50th sale |
| Listing a product | free |

The commission is deducted from each payout. It is not refunded when a customer
returns an item for a reason other than a defect (see "Returns and refunds",
section 5); it is refunded when the return is caused by a defect, damage or a
wrong item.

## 2. Payouts

Albert's Marketplace pays you **every 14 days**, by bank transfer, for every
order delivered at least 7 days before the payout date, minus commissions,
subscription and refunds. A payout below €10 is carried over to the next one.

## 3. Shipping your orders

You ship every order yourself, with one of our four carriers (Albatros Parcels,
Blue Heron Logistics, Kestrel Freight, Swift Otter Delivery), within **2
business days** of the order. The customer sees an estimated delivery date 7
days after the order; ship so that it is met.

## 4. Answering customers and returns

You answer customer messages within **2 business days**. You confirm a return
within 3 business days of receiving the parcel; if you do not answer within 7
business days, Albert's Marketplace confirms it on your behalf and refunds the
customer from your next payout.

## 5. Product pages

The product page is the customer's only source of information before buying,
and our assistant uses it to answer customers' questions. You are responsible
for everything you write in it. A product page:

- describes the product accurately: its ingredients, its size, what it does;
- states the product's real condition and authenticity;
- **contains no instructions to customers, to our staff or to any automated
  system**, no links to other websites, and no contact details: customers
  contact you through Albert's Marketplace only;
- never states a return or refund policy different from Albert's Marketplace's.

A product page that breaks these rules is removed, and a repeated or deliberate
breach suspends your account.

## 6. Prohibited products

You may not sell counterfeit products, products past their expiry date,
products whose seal has been broken, prescription medicines, or any product
whose sale is restricted in one of the six countries we deliver to. A product
reported as not authentic is removed while our trust and safety team examines
it; if the report is upheld, the customer is refunded from your next payout and
your account may be closed.

## 7. Reviews

You may not offer anything in exchange for a review, write reviews of your own
products, or ask a customer to change or remove a review. You can reply to a
review once, publicly.
